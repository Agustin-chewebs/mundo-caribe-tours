Holbox: Island and Nature Adventure

⏱ About 10 hours 📅 Monday, Wednesday, and Friday

Explore Holbox, a paradise of white sand and crystal-clear water. Swim in the refreshing Yalahau spring, discover the beauty of Isla de la Pasión, and stroll the colorful streets of Holbox by bike or on foot.

Included

  • Round-trip transportation
  • Box lunch
  • Drinks on board
  • À la carte meal + 1 non-alcoholic drink
  • Bike rental (1 hour)
  • Swim at the Yalahau spring
  • Visit to Isla de la Pasión
  • Free time in Holbox
Fee (not included): $35 MXN, paid directly
